About Me

I am Amir Ebadivand, a mechanical engineer holding an M.Sc. in Energy Conversion from the University of Tabriz. My experience spans mechanical equipment design & drafting, HVAC systems, hands-on CNC machining and supervising industrial part production. I have worked professionally with AutoCAD and Carrier HAP, and I'm passionate about passing my technical knowledge to a new generation of engineers.

I believe real learning happens through practice and projects. That's why my courses are designed so students face real industry challenges from day one.
